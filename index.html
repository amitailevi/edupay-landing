Two rails for education institutions

Families and institutions settle stable value. SEDU is the ecosystem and fundraising layer — not a place to put school budgets into a volatile token.

SEDU

Utility, community, fundraising

SolEdu (SEDU) is a Solana token that aligns supporters with the product and funds what comes next. It is not the balance schools should hold for payroll, rent, or tuition already collected.

USD-pegged

Settlement that holds its value

Tuition, fees, and institutional balances use a dollar-stable rail — Solana USDC or an institution-facing USD-pegged token — so schools and academic institutions do not take FX or crypto volatility risk on money meant for operations.

How institutions use it

Registration and collections for schools, after-school programs, and academic institutions internationally. SEDU does not power live billing today.

Registration and collections

Programs enroll families and collect fees without a pile of disconnected forms and payment pages.

Transparent movement

On-chain records where they help operators and communities see what moved. Day-to-day balances stay on the dollar-pegged rail.

Operator dashboards

Enrollment, invoices, and payment status across branches — the operating picture from education-operator practice. No private phone numbers or passwords are published here.

The problem

After-school and education operators still run money through disconnected tools. Parents cannot see a clear status, and multi-site programs break when the back office stays manual.

Disconnected tools

Forms, spreadsheets, chat threads, and one-off payment pages do not share one record of who registered and who paid.

Parents lack status

Families should see whether a registration is confirmed and a payment is received, without chasing an inbox.

Money is hard to audit

When collections live in side channels, operators and communities cannot trace what came in or what it funded.

Multi-site scale breaks

Multi-branch programs, invoices, and payment status do not survive copy-paste operations across sites.

That operating picture — multi-tenant registration, billing, invoices, and back-office for education operators — is the real-world problem SEDU aims to settle on-chain later. The token does not power live billing today.
