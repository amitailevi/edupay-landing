The problem

After-school and education operators still run money through disconnected tools. Parents cannot see a clear status, and multi-site programs break when the back office stays manual.

Disconnected tools

Forms, spreadsheets, chat threads, and one-off payment pages do not share one record of who registered and who paid.

Parents lack status

Families should see whether a registration is confirmed and a payment is received, without chasing an inbox.

Money is hard to audit

When collections live in side channels, operators and communities cannot trace what came in or what it funded.

Multi-site scale breaks

Multi-branch programs, invoices, and payment status do not survive copy-paste operations across sites.

That operating picture — multi-tenant registration, billing, invoices, and back-office for education operators — is the real-world problem SEDU aims to settle on-chain later. The token does not power live billing today.

What SEDU is

Infrastructure for education payments and transparent fundraising on Solana. Less friction in collections, more transparency in money movement.

Phase 1 — Raise

A transparent community raise to fund the product. Follow this page for the official mint and trade links only.

Phase 2 — Rails

Later settlement for registration and collections between families, schools and other institutions, and operators — including academic programs internationally.

Not advice

What we are building

Raise first, then ship modules. Nothing here is a claim that SEDU already runs a live billing product.

Registration and collections

A path for programs to take registrations and collect fees without a pile of separate payment pages.

Operator dashboards

Back-office views for multi-branch programs: who is enrolled, what is invoiced, and what is paid.

Parent payment clarity

A status families can check, instead of asking in chat whether a payment landed.

Fundraising transparency

On-chain visibility for the community raise that funds those modules.

Why Solana

The network is the rail. BTC and SOL in the market strip are public references for that market — not a SEDU price.

Fast confirmation

Payments and transfers confirm quickly enough for real registration and collection flows.

Low fees

Small network fees keep education payments practical, including smaller program charges.

Global rails

Cross-border by default, for institutions and operators that are not limited to one country.
